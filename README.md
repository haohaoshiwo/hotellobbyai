## Made for simple, memorable moments

- Bring friends, couples, family members, creators, or a person and their pet into one short video.
- Choose a vertical 9:16 frame for mobile sharing or a 16:9 frame for wider posts.
- Preview and download your completed video when it is ready.
- Buy Credits only when you need them—no recurring subscription.

## How it works

1. Choose two clear photos, one for each subject.
2. Select the frame that fits where you plan to share.
3. Create your video, then preview and download the finished result.
